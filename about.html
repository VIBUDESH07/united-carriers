About us

We're building something long-term.

Madhusree Private Limited is an India-based import–export company focused on connecting quality Indian agricultural products with international markets.

Who we are

Spices first, with turmeric at the heart.

Our initial focus is spices, with turmeric at the heart of our product portfolio alongside cumin seeds, coriander seeds, red chilli and black pepper.

We believe successful international trade begins with good products, clear communication and relationships built for the long term. As we grow, our aim is to develop dependable sourcing relationships, understand buyer requirements carefully and represent Indian products with professionalism in global markets.

✓

Our vision

To build a trusted international business that connects the quality and diversity of Indian agricultural products with buyers around the world.

✓

Our mission

To source responsibly, communicate clearly, serve buyers professionally and build long-term trade relationships through consistent product and service.

✓

Where we are

An early-stage business, building its sourcing and buyer relationships product by product rather than claiming scale it has not yet earned.

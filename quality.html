Quality & sourcing

Quality begins at the source.

For an international buyer, quality is more than appearance. It includes consistency, handling, packaging, documentation and alignment with destination-market requirements.

Our sourcing approach is built around understanding the specification first and then coordinating supply around that requirement.

01

Sourcing

We identify suitable supply based on product, quantity, quality requirements and commercial terms.

02

Selection

We pay attention to agreed product characteristics and avoid treating every requirement as interchangeable.

03

Handling & storage

Products should be handled and stored using appropriate practices for the specific commodity and packaging format.

04

Testing & documentation

Where required and available, relevant product testing and documentation is coordinated against buyer and destination requirements.

05

Packaging

Packaging is discussed according to product, quantity, transport conditions and buyer requirements.

06

Export readiness

Before shipment, commercial, product and shipping details are reviewed so the order is prepared against the agreed requirement.
